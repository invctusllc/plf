Cannabis Product Types

Cannabis products come in many different forms, and consumption methods can vary based on product type.

Explore product types by category to build awareness about which product types are right for you.

Experience PowerLeaf See How PowerLeaf Works

Flower & pre rolls. Heated and inhaled. Quick onset and short lasting effects.

Vapes & concentrates. Vape oils, rosin, crumble, budder, shatter, and more. Heated and inhaled. Potent, quick onset, short lasting effects. Most suitable for experienced users.

Edibles & beverages. Ingested like food. Slow onset and long lasting effects. Discreet.

Tinctures & lozenges. Absorbed in the mouth or under the tongue. Quicker onset than edibles. Long lasting effects. Discreet.

Topicals & transdermal patches. Lotions, creams, and balms for nonintoxicating, localized relief. Transdermal patches for steady dosing and long lasting effects (intoxicating).

Capsules. Ingested like pills. Convenient, precise dosing. Slow onset, long lasting effects. Discreet.
